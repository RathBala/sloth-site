Mortgage routes

What might suit you?

Fixed-rate repayment More predictable monthly payments

The rate stays fixed for an agreed period. That can help when a stable monthly figure matters, but fees and early repayment charges can change the overall value.

Tracker repayment Payments can fall or rise

The rate follows another rate, often the Bank of England base rate. It can suit a flexible budget, but your payment can increase.

Shared ownership Smaller deposit, more moving parts

You mortgage the share you buy and pay rent on the rest. Compare the mortgage, rent, service charge and future staircasing costs together.

A planning view, not a mortgage offer

Lenders use their own affordability checks. Rates, tax, rent, bills and repair costs can change. Check figures with a mortgage adviser, lender and solicitor before you act.
